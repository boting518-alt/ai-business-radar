## Mandatory dimension eligibility before drafting

Check persisted Signal classifications before writing any supported dimension. Do not reclassify
Signals during synthesis, even if prose sounds related. A supported or partially_supported field
must cite at least one eligible Signal AND the actual excerpt must substantiate the claim:
- pricing_summary: signal_type=pricing and evidence_role=product_pricing or observed_transaction.
- revenue_summary: signal_type=revenue and evidence_role=product_monetization or observed_transaction.
  Never cite creator_monetization for product revenue.
- adoption_summary: signal_type=adoption, excluding seller_cta and creator_monetization.
  Purchase intent, reported orders, preorders, demand and sales revenue are NOT adoption Signals.
  If there is no eligible adoption Signal, return unknown even when orders or sales are reported.
  Preserve appropriately qualified buyer comments in customer_summary and validation_gaps instead.
- competition_summary: signal_type=competition. Multiple named products or adjacent examples alone
  are not competition evidence; retain these examples in solution_pattern and return unknown here.
- business_model_summary: explicit business_model or evidence_role=product_monetization,
  product_pricing or observed_transaction, plus an explicit monetization structure in source evidence.
  Preorder availability alone is an ordering state, not a business model. Return unknown in that case.
- distribution_summary: signal_type=distribution or nonempty distribution_channels, PLUS an explicit
  acquisition/channel mechanism in the actual excerpt. Inherited extraction metadata is not enough.
  Preorder availability alone, an unrelated YouTube channel, or a metadata-only channel is unknown.

These gates are necessary but not sufficient: envelope metadata, current human fields, and taxonomy
must never manufacture source support. If no eligible citation exists, use null text, empty IDs,
unknown information_class and insufficient_evidence support_level, with the concrete validation gap.
Before returning, inspect every commercial dimension against these gates and remove unsupported prose.
